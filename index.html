In my free time I'm a creative writer, working primarily on fiction these days. I'm currently finishing up three stories:

The Bell Station

Operators at a living monitoring station tend an unknowable signal from the void. Two of them begin to remember a life they shared before through a memory of apple pie. Then, the station starts turning toward something new in the dark.

For Magic Eyes Only

Five generals of a Jupiterean order arrive at Mound 6, the last standing imperial position on the western frontier. They are tasked with destroying the rebels in the marsh before them, but one among them is a traitor.

A Pale Horse on God's Tongue

Eco-vigilantes aboard The Shaibya hunt the world's largest refrigerated fishing carrier across lawless international waters. They track their leviathan by the dead albatross it leaves floating in its wake.
